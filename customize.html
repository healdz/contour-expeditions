Built Around You

Your Expedition, Your Way

Dreaming of a ski traverse in Georgia's Caucasus, a first 6,000-metre peak in Bolivia, or a packrafting route through Patagonia? We help independent adventurers, clubs, and private groups plan expeditions anywhere in the world.

We'll work with you on route planning, timing, permits, and logistics, then connect you with vetted international guiding organizations and local outfitters where you want them. You can have us plan every detail, or just the parts you'd rather not untangle yourself.

The Process

How It Works

  1. 1

    Tell Us Your Idea

    Share where, when, and what kind of adventure using the form below.

  2. 2

    Planning Call

    A free 30-minute call to discuss goals, experience, and budget.

  3. 3

    Route & Guide Matching

    We draft an itinerary and introduce you to trusted guides and outfitters.

  4. 4

    You Go

    Bookings confirmed, GPX and notes delivered, and 24/7 support while you're out.
