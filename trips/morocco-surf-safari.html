Moroccan Surf Safari

Self-drive the Atlantic Coast

30.54°N 9.71°WMax elev. Sea level10 Days
Dates & Prices View Itinerary
Overview

Morocco's Atlantic coast is lined with long right-hand point breaks, quiet beach breaks, and fishing villages where the surf culture runs deep. Pick up your van and boards in Agadir, then follow our spot guide north to Imsouane and Essaouira and south to the red-cliff beaches of Mirleft and Sidi Ifni. We pre-book surf guesthouses and riads, send daily forecast notes, and set you up with a local surf guide on day two so you know which spots work on which swell.

Day by Day

Trip Itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Agadir and Taghazout

Collect your vehicle and boards, then drive 30 minutes to Taghazout.

Day 2Taghazout: local guide session

A half-day session with a local surf guide covering the lineups, tides, and etiquette.

Day 3Taghazout and Paradise Valley

Surf the dawn session, then visit the palm-lined pools of Paradise Valley.

Day 4Drive to Imsouane~90 km

Head north to the bay that holds one of Africa's longest right-hand waves.

Day 5Imsouane

Long rides on the bay, with sunset tagine in the village.

Day 6Essaouira and Sidi Kaouki~110 km

Wander the UNESCO-listed medina and surf Sidi Kaouki's beach breaks.

Day 7Back south to Tamraght~170 km

Devil's Rock and Banana Point for mellower waves. Hammam evening.

Day 8Drive to Mirleft~160 km

Quiet beaches and dramatic cliffs. Visit the Legzira arches.

Day 9Sidi Ifni~35 km

Art-deco town with uncrowded lefts and rights.

Day 10Return to Agadir and depart~160 km

Drop off the vehicle and boards at the airport.

Common Questions

How fit do I need to be?

You should be able to paddle out and catch unbroken waves on your own; this trip suits intermediate surfers. Beginners can add lessons in Taghazout, so get in touch and we'll tailor the plan.

Can I do this trip solo?

Yes, though we recommend at least two people for safety and cost. Solo travellers pay a single supplement for accommodation, and we schedule an extra check-in call each day.

What's included in the trip?

See the full list under What's Included above. In short: 9 nights in surf guesthouses and riads with breakfast; rental van with roof racks (2 drivers); two boards and a wetsuit per surfer for the whole trip. Flights and travel insurance are not included.

What support do I get on the trip?

You'll have a pre-trip video briefing, GPX tracks and route notes, and a 24/7 support line that connects to our team. We can rebook accommodation, arrange transfers, or coordinate with local rescue services if anything changes.

What happens after I book?

You'll receive a welcome pack with packing list, travel logistics, and recommended training within 48 hours, followed by a planning call with your trip lead about 8 weeks before departure.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable up to 90 days before departure. Within 90 days, payments can be transferred to a future trip. Full terms are provided when you book.
