Overview

The Dolomites are made for mixing it up. Based in Cortina d'Ampezzo and Val Gardena, this trip pairs the classic hike around Tre Cime di Lavaredo with via ferratas through First World War tunnels, a night in a mountain rifugio at 2,752 m, a bike loop over the four passes of the Sella Ronda, and a guided introduction to Dolomite limestone. Evenings are spent eating South Tyrolean and Ladin food in boutique alpine hotels.

Day by Day

Trip Itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Cortina d'Ampezzo1,224 m

Private transfer from Venice. Welcome dinner in town.

Day 2Tre Cime di Lavaredo circuit2,454 m

Hike the iconic loop around the three towers, with lunch at Rifugio Locatelli.

Day 3Lagazuoi via ferrata2,778 m

Climb through restored WWI tunnels to the summit. Overnight at Rifugio Lagazuoi for sunset and sunrise.

Day 4Rock climbing at Passo Sella2,240 m

Descend, then transfer to Val Gardena. Afternoon guided climbing session — beginners welcome.

Day 5Sella Ronda by bike~55 km

Ride the four-pass loop around the Sella massif on road or e-bikes, with plenty of coffee stops.

Day 6Seceda ridge and Puez-Odle2,519 m

Ride the lift to Seceda and hike the dramatic ridgeline above Val Gardena.

Day 7Via ferrata choice day

Choose between a classic via ferrata on the Sella group or a relaxed hike. Farewell dinner.

Day 8Depart

Private transfer to Venice.

Common Questions

How fit do I need to be?

You should be comfortable hiking 5–6 hours with up to 900 m of ascent. No climbing or via ferrata experience is needed; guides cover technique. E-bikes make the Sella Ronda accessible to most riders.

Can I join solo?

Absolutely — most of our guests travel solo. We pair solo travellers into shared rooms or tents, or you can add a single supplement.

What's included in the trip?

See the full list under What's Included above. In short: certified mountain guide for via ferrata and climbing days; cycling guide and road or e-bike rental; via ferrata kit, harness, and helmet. Flights and travel insurance are not included.

What happens after I book?

You'll receive a welcome pack with packing list, travel logistics, and recommended training within 48 hours, followed by a planning call with your trip lead about 8 weeks before departure.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable up to 90 days before departure. Within 90 days, payments can be transferred to a future trip. Full terms are provided when you book.
