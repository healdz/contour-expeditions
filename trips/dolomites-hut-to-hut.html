Dolomites Hut to Hut

Self-guided Alta Via 1

46.69°N 12.09°EMax elev. 2,752 m9 Days
Dates & Prices View Itinerary
Overview

The Alta Via 1 is the classic Dolomites traverse, weaving south from the emerald waters of Lago di Braies, across the Fanes plateau and under the walls of the Civetta, to the edge of the range near Belluno. Hut bookings on this route sell out months in advance — we handle every reservation, provide detailed route notes and GPX tracks, and stay on call while you walk it independently.

Day by Day

Trip Itinerary

Day 1Arrive at Lago di Braies1,496 m

Make your way to the lakeside hotel. Evening video check-in with our team.

Day 2Braies to Rifugio Sennes2,116 m

Climb past Lago di Braies to the Sennes plateau.

Day 3Sennes to Rifugio Lagazuoi2,752 m

Cross the Fanes plateau and climb to the highest hut on the route.

Day 4Lagazuoi to Rifugio Nuvolau2,575 m

Pass the Cinque Torri open-air WWI museum to a summit hut with 360° views.

Day 5Nuvolau to Rifugio Coldai2,135 m

Descend to Passo Giau and continue below Monte Pelmo.

Day 6Coldai to Rifugio Vazzoler1,714 m

One of the great days of the route, walking beneath the 1,200 m north-west wall of the Civetta.

Day 7Vazzoler to Passo Duran1,601 m

Wooded trails and quiet valleys to a family-run pass hut.

Day 8Passo Duran to Rifugio Pramperet1,857 m

Enter the wilder Dolomiti Bellunesi National Park.

Day 9Descend to Belluno

Final descent to La Pissa and bus to Belluno, with trains to Venice.

Common Questions

How fit do I need to be?

Expect 5–7 hours of walking a day with 700–1,100 m of ascent, carrying a 7–9 kg pack. Trails are well marked, with a few short exposed sections protected by cables.

Can I do this trip solo?

Yes, though we recommend at least two people for safety and cost. Solo travellers pay a single supplement for accommodation, and we schedule an extra check-in call each day.

What's included in the trip?

See the full list under What's Included above. In short: all rifugi pre-booked with half board (dinner and breakfast); first-night hotel at Lago di Braies; detailed route notes and GPX files for every stage. Flights and travel insurance are not included.

What support do I get on the trip?

You'll have a pre-trip video briefing, GPX tracks and route notes, and a 24/7 support line that connects to our team. We can rebook accommodation, arrange transfers, or coordinate with local rescue services if anything changes.

What happens after I book?

You'll receive a welcome pack with packing list, travel logistics, and recommended training within 48 hours, followed by a planning call with your trip lead about 8 weeks before departure.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable up to 90 days before departure. Within 90 days, payments can be transferred to a future trip. Full terms are provided when you book.
