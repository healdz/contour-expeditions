Peruvian Trekking

The Huayhuash Circuit

10.27°S 76.91°WMax elev. 5,020 m13 Days
Dates & Prices View Itinerary
Overview

Often called the most beautiful trek in the world, the Huayhuash circuit loops a compact, jagged range of 6,000-metre peaks above Peru's Andean highlands. We take the time to acclimatize properly in Huaraz, then spend nine days on trail with arrieros and burros carrying the load — crossing passes over 5,000 m, camping beside glacial lakes beneath Yerupajá, and soaking in natural hot springs at Viconga.

Day by Day

Trip Itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Huaraz3,050 m

Travel from Lima to Huaraz, the mountain capital of the Cordillera Blanca. Welcome dinner and gear check with your guides.

Day 2Acclimatization: Laguna Wilcacocha3,700 m

An easy half-day hike with sweeping views across to Huascarán and the Cordillera Blanca.

Day 3Acclimatization: Laguna 694,600 m

A classic day hike in Huascarán National Park to a startlingly blue lake beneath Chacraraju's glaciers.

Day 4Drive to Cuartelhuain4,170 m

Scenic drive via Chiquián to the trailhead. Meet the arriero team and settle into camp.

Day 5Cacanán Pass to Mitucocha4,690 m pass

Cross the Continental Divide on the first pass of the circuit and descend to camp near Laguna Mitucocha.

Day 6Carhuac Pass to Carhuacocha4,630 m pass

Arrive at one of the most photographed campsites in the Andes, with Yerupajá and Siula Grande reflected in the lake.

Day 7Siula Pass and the Three Lakes4,830 m pass

Climb past three glacial lakes to Siula Pass — the backdrop to 'Touching the Void' — then descend to Huayhuash camp.

Day 8Portachuelo Pass to Viconga4,750 m pass

A gentler day ending at the Viconga hot springs. Soak tired legs in natural pools.

Day 9Punta Cuyoc5,000 m pass

The highest pass of the classic route, with views over the Cordillera Raura. Camp in the Huanacpatay valley.

Day 10Huayllapa to Gashpapampa4,500 m

Descend through the village of Huayllapa, then climb steadily to a high camp.

Day 11Yaucha Pass to Jahuacocha4,750 m pass

Traverse beneath Rondoy and Ninashanca to Laguna Jahuacocha — a favourite final camp.

Day 12Pampa Llamac Pass and return4,300 m pass

Final pass with a last panorama of the range, descend to Llamac and drive back to Huaraz for a celebration dinner.

Day 13Depart

Transfer back toward Lima for onward flights.

Common Questions

How fit do I need to be?

You should be comfortable hiking 6–8 hours a day for several days in a row with 600–1,000 m of ascent. No technical skills are required, but altitude is the real challenge — we build in two acclimatization days and a slow pace.

Can I join solo?

Absolutely — most of our guests travel solo. We pair solo travellers into shared rooms or tents, or you can add a single supplement.

What's included in the trip?

See the full list under What's Included above. In short: certified local mountain guide and Contour trip leader; arrieros and burros to carry group and personal gear; all meals on trek • breakfasts in Huaraz. Flights and travel insurance are not included.

What happens after I book?

You'll receive a welcome pack with packing list, travel logistics, and recommended training within 48 hours, followed by a planning call with your trip lead about 8 weeks before departure.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable up to 90 days before departure. Within 90 days, payments can be transferred to a future trip. Full terms are provided when you book.
